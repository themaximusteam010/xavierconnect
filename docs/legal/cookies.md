**Effective date:** [TO COMPLETE]

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. XavierConnect uses only the cookies the site needs to work. **We do not use advertising, tracking or third-party analytics cookies.**

| Cookie | Purpose | How long |
|---|---|---|
| `xc_session` | Keeps you signed in securely | 30 days, or until you sign out |
| `xc_cart` | Remembers your cart if you are not signed in | 30 days |
| `xc_oauth` | Protects the Google sign-in step against forgery | 10 minutes |

We also use your browser's local storage (not cookies) to remember **recently viewed products** (`xc_recent`) and **products you chose to compare** (`xc_compare`). This information stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it in your browser settings.

Because these cookies are strictly necessary to provide the service you ask for, they do not require consent. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but sign-in, cart and checkout will not work without them.

Payment pages run by our payment provider may set their own cookies, which are governed by that provider's policy.

If we ever add optional cookies, such as analytics, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

Questions: privacy@xavierconnect.com. See also our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).
