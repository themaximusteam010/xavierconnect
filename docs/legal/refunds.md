---
title: Refund Policy
description: When and how buyers are refunded on XavierConnect.
---
**Effective date:** 26 November 2026

Your payment is held by XavierConnect and is not released to the Seller until delivery is confirmed and a short holding period has passed. That is what makes refunds possible.

## 1. When you get a refund

You are refunded for the affected Seller's order (items and delivery charge, unless stated otherwise below) when:

- the Seller cancels or cannot fulfil the order;
- the order is not delivered and the Seller cannot show proof of delivery;
- an approved return is received back by the Seller (see the [Return Policy](/legal/returns));
- a dispute is decided in your favour, in full or in part;
- we cancel the order for security or legal reasons.

## 2. How refunds are paid

- Refunds go back to **the original payment method**. We cannot refund to a different person or account.
- We start the refund as soon as it is approved. Your bank or card issuer typically takes **5–10 business days** to show it, which is outside our control.
- Refunds are in naira, for the amount you paid for the refunded items. We do not charge refund fees.

## 3. Partial refunds

A partial refund may be agreed or decided when, for example, only some items in an order are affected, or an item is returned used, damaged by the buyer, or incomplete.

## 4. Delivery charges

Delivery charges are refunded when the Seller cancels, the item never arrived, or the item was faulty, damaged or not as described. If you return an item because you changed your mind, and the Seller accepts change-of-mind returns, the original delivery charge and the cost of sending it back are usually yours, unless the listing says otherwise.

## 5. Digital products and services

- **Digital products** are refundable if the file is faulty, cannot be downloaded, or is materially different from its description, or if the listing allows refunds. Once a working file has been downloaded, change-of-mind refunds are not available unless the listing says so.
- **Services** follow the cancellation terms on the listing. If the Seller does not turn up or does not provide the service, you get a full refund.

## 6. Time limits

Report a problem from your order page as soon as possible, and **before the Seller has been paid**. After that, we may not be able to recover the money through the platform, although you keep your legal rights against the Seller.

## 7. Abuse

We may refuse refunds and suspend accounts where there is evidence of fraud, such as false claims of non-delivery, swapping items or repeated abuse of returns.

Nothing in this policy affects your rights under the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018 or other Nigerian law.
