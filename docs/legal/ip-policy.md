---
title: Intellectual Property Policy
description: How to report listings that infringe trademarks, copyright or other rights.
---
**Effective date:** 26 November 2026

XavierConnect respects intellectual property rights and expects Sellers to do the same. Sellers are responsible for making sure their listings, images, descriptions, products and digital files do not infringe anyone's trademark, copyright, design or other rights.

## Reporting an infringement

If you own rights that you believe a listing infringes, email **support@xavierconnect.com** with the subject "IP complaint" and include:

1. your name, contact details and your relationship to the rights owner;
2. a description of the right (for example the trademark registration number, or the copyrighted work);
3. a link to each listing concerned;
4. why you believe it infringes;
5. a statement that the information is accurate and that you are the rights owner or authorised to act for them.

We will review the complaint and may remove or disable the listing while we investigate. We will tell the Seller and give them the chance to respond with evidence that they are authorised (for example a licence or proof of genuine origin).

## Repeat infringers

Accounts that repeatedly infringe will be suspended or closed, and payouts related to infringing sales may be withheld.

## False complaints

Do not submit complaints you know to be false. False complaints may result in the loss of access to this reporting process, and you may be liable for the damage they cause.
