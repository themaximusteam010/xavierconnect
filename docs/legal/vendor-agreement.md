**Effective date:** [TO COMPLETE]

This agreement is between **Xavier Enterprise** (operator of XavierConnect, "**we**") and you, the seller ("**Seller**", "**you**"). It applies in addition to our [Terms and Conditions](/legal/terms) and the policies linked from them. By applying to sell or listing anything, you accept it.

## 1. Approval and verification

1.1 You must complete verification (identity, business and payout details) and be approved before selling. We may ask for more documents at any time, and may refuse or withdraw approval if information is false, incomplete or raises fraud, legal or reputational concerns.

1.2 You confirm that you are legally allowed to carry on your business and sell your products or services, and that you hold every licence, registration, certification and permission required (for example NAFDAC registration where applicable).

## 2. Your listings

2.1 You are the seller of record and solely responsible for your listings and products. Listings must be accurate, complete and lawful, including price, condition, specifications, origin, weight and dimensions, stock and delivery time. Photos must show the actual item or a fair representation of it.

2.2 You must not list anything in our [Prohibited Items Policy](/legal/prohibited-items), counterfeit or infringing goods, or items you do not have the right to sell.

2.3 We may edit listing formatting, and may hide, remove or refuse any listing that breaks this agreement or the law, or that receives credible complaints.

2.4 **No fake activity.** You must not post or buy reviews, review your own items, or inflate sales or ratings.

## 3. Orders and fulfilment

3.1 Accept or decline orders promptly, and dispatch within your stated handling time.

3.2 Ship with tracking where possible, and record the courier and tracking number on the order. When you book a courier through XavierConnect, use the delivery option the buyer paid for.

3.3 International Sellers must declare customs contents and values truthfully. Under-declaring or misdescribing goods is an offence, and you are responsible for any resulting seizure, fine or loss.

3.4 You must honour our [Return Policy](/legal/returns), [Refund Policy](/legal/refunds) and dispute decisions, and respond to disputes within **3 days**.

3.5 You must not ask buyers to pay, or communicate, outside XavierConnect, and must not use buyers' personal data for anything other than fulfilling and supporting their order.

## 4. Fees

4.1 We charge a **commission** on each completed sale, calculated on the item price plus delivery charge (less any discount you fund), at the rate for your plan or category at the time of the order. Current default rates: **Starter 10%, Growth 8%, Business 6.5%, Enterprise negotiated**. Subscription plan fees are shown on the plans page.

4.2 Courier labels bought through XavierConnect are charged at the price shown when you book.

4.3 We will give at least **30 days' notice** of fee increases. Changes do not apply to orders placed before they take effect.

4.4 You are responsible for your own taxes, including VAT where you are registered, and for issuing any invoices the law requires.

## 5. Payments and settlement

5.1 We collect payment from buyers on your behalf. Your earnings (sale amount less commission, fees, refunds and adjustments) become available only after delivery is confirmed and a holding period has passed: currently **3 days** after independently evidenced delivery (carrier scan, rider proof, buyer confirmation, or digital delivery), and **14 days** when the only evidence is your own "delivered" confirmation.

5.2 Earnings are held while a dispute or return on the order is open, and may be held during an investigation of suspected fraud or a breach of this agreement.

5.3 Payouts go only to a verified account in your name or your registered business's name, above the minimum payout (currently **₦5,000**). We will never ask for your bank login, PIN or password.

5.4 We may deduct from your balance: refunds owed to buyers on your orders, chargebacks, fines or costs caused by your breach, and any amount paid to you in error. If your balance is not enough, you must repay the shortfall within 14 days of our request.

5.5 Our records of transactions, calculated from our ledger, are evidence of the amounts due unless shown to be wrong. Report any error within 60 days of the transaction.

## 6. Your liability and indemnity

6.1 You are responsible to buyers for your products and services, including their quality, safety, description, fitness for purpose and compliance with consumer protection and product safety law.

6.2 You will compensate Xavier Enterprise for losses, claims, fines and reasonable costs (including reasonable legal fees) arising from your products, listings, services, content, breach of this agreement, or breach of law, including intellectual property infringement and data protection breaches.

6.3 Our total liability to you under this agreement in any 12-month period is limited to the commission you paid us in that period, except where the law does not allow such a limit. We are not liable for loss of profit, business or goodwill, or for indirect losses.

## 7. Intellectual property

You give us a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use your store name, logo, listing text and images to operate and promote XavierConnect, including in search results, advertising and social media. You confirm that you have the rights to grant this.

## 8. Suspension and termination

8.1 You may close your store with 14 days' notice, after you have completed open orders, returns, disputes and payouts.

8.2 We may suspend listings, payouts or your account immediately, or terminate this agreement, if you break it, receive repeated well-founded complaints, show high cancellation or dispute rates, are suspected of fraud, or if we are required to by law. Where lawful, we will tell you the reason and how to respond.

8.3 After termination, balances due to you are paid after any holding periods, deductions and investigations are complete. Sections 5.4, 6 and 7 continue after termination.

## 9. Data protection

For buyers' data you receive, you act as an independent controller under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. You must keep it secure, use it only to fulfil and support the order, and delete it when it is no longer needed for that purpose or for legal record-keeping.

## 10. Changes

We may update this agreement. We will give at least **14 days' notice** of material changes (30 days for fee increases). If you continue selling after a change takes effect, you accept the change.

## 11. General

This agreement is governed by Nigerian law. Disputes go first to good-faith negotiation, then to mediation in Lagos, then to the courts of Nigeria. You are an independent business; this agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.
