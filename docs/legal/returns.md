## 1. What can be returned

You can request a return of a physical item within **7 days of delivery** if it:

- arrived damaged or defective;
- is the wrong item;
- is materially different from its description or photos;
- is missing parts or accessories.

**Change-of-mind returns** are accepted only where the Seller's listing says so.

These items cannot be returned unless they are faulty or not as described: perishable food, personal-care and hygiene items whose seal has been broken, underwear and swimwear, custom-made or personalised items, digital products that have been downloaded, and services already performed.

## 2. How to return an item

1. Go to **Account → Shipping & tracking**, find the order and choose **Request a return**. Give the reason, and send photos to the Seller in Messages if they help.
2. The Seller approves or rejects the request. If it is approved, you receive the return instructions. XavierConnect can step in if the Seller does not respond.
3. Send the item back securely packed, in the condition you received it, with all parts and accessories. Add the courier name and tracking number on your Shipping & tracking page. Keep your proof of postage.
4. When the Seller confirms they have received it, your refund starts automatically (see the [Refund Policy](/legal/refunds)).

## 3. Who pays for return shipping

- **Faulty, damaged, wrong or not-as-described items:** the Seller pays for the return. Keep your receipt so it can be reimbursed with your refund.
- **Change-of-mind returns (where allowed):** you pay for the return unless the listing says otherwise.

## 4. If a return is rejected

The Seller must give a reason. If you disagree, open a dispute from your order page and XavierConnect will review the evidence from both sides.

## 5. Seller payments during a return

While a return is open, the Seller is not paid for that order.

Nothing in this policy affects your rights under Nigerian consumer protection law.
