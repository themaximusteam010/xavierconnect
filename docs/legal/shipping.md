---
title: Shipping and Delivery Policy
description: How delivery works on XavierConnect, including international orders.
---
**Effective date:** 26 November 2026

## 1. Every Seller ships separately

XavierConnect is a marketplace. Each Seller ships their own items from their own location, so one checkout can arrive as several parcels, on different days, with separate tracking. Items from different Sellers are never combined into one parcel.

## 2. Delivery options at checkout

For each Seller you choose either:

- **a courier**, priced live by the courier company (a XavierConnect service fee may be included in the price shown). Prices are valid for a limited time and for the delivery address entered; or
- **the Seller's own delivery**, at a price the Seller sets.

You always see the delivery price for each Seller before you pay.

## 3. Delivery times

Delivery estimates come from the courier or the Seller. They are **estimates, not guarantees**, and can change because of weather, traffic, public holidays, security issues, courier capacity or customs. XavierConnect does not set them itself. Sellers dispatch within the handling time shown on their store.

## 4. Tracking

When your order is dispatched, you get a notification. Tracking details appear under **Account → Shipping & tracking**. Courier tracking updates appear automatically where the courier provides them.

## 5. Your responsibilities

Enter a complete and correct address and a reachable phone number, and be available to receive the parcel. If delivery fails because of an incorrect address or because you could not be reached, re-delivery or return charges may apply.

## 6. Lost, damaged or late parcels

- Check your parcel on arrival. If it is damaged, take photos and report it from your order page as soon as possible.
- If a parcel is lost, or not delivered within a reasonable time after the estimate, open a dispute. Your payment is still held, and you will be refunded if the Seller cannot show proof of delivery.

## 7. International orders (for example China → Nigeria)

- Some Sellers are outside Nigeria. Their items may travel through a consolidation warehouse and by air or sea freight with one of our freight partners, before customs clearance and final delivery in Nigeria.
- Air freight typically takes about 1–3 weeks and sea freight about 6–10 weeks. These are only typical ranges; dates shown on your order come from the freight partner or courier.
- **Import duties, taxes and customs charges** are set by the Nigeria Customs Service, not by XavierConnect. Unless the listing or checkout says they are included, they may be payable by you. If any amount is due from you, you will be told the official amount before you pay it. We never estimate or invent customs amounts.
- Items can be held, inspected or refused by customs. If an item is confiscated because it is prohibited, and the Seller should have known this, you will be refunded from the Seller's held payment.

## 8. Restricted items

Some items cannot be shipped at all, or only with restrictions. See the [Prohibited Items Policy](/legal/prohibited-items).
