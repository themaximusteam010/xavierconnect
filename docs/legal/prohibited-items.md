title: Prohibited Items Policy
description: Items and services that cannot be listed or sold on XavierConnect.
---
**Effective date:** [TO COMPLETE]

Sellers are responsible for making sure everything they list is legal to sell in Nigeria and, for international Sellers, legal to export and import. The following must not be listed on XavierConnect:

## Never allowed

- Firearms, ammunition, explosives, fireworks and weapon parts; knives or weapons designed to cause harm
- Illegal drugs, drug paraphernalia, and prescription-only medicines
- Counterfeit, replica or "inspired by" branded goods, and pirated media, software or licence keys
- Stolen goods, and goods whose ownership cannot be shown
- Human remains, body parts, and protected animals or plants and products made from them
- Hazardous materials: flammable, corrosive, toxic or radioactive substances, and gas cylinders
- Items listed on the Nigeria Customs Service import or export prohibition lists, where they are being imported or exported
- Currency, gift cards bought fraudulently, financial instruments, investment schemes, loans, and "money doubling" schemes
- Personal data, accounts, logins, hacking tools, malware and spy software
- Adult sexual content and services, and anything that exploits minors
- Government documents and IDs, uniforms or badges used to impersonate officials
- Items promoting hate, violence or terrorism
- Recalled or unsafe products

## Restricted (allowed only with approval and proof)

- Food, drinks, cosmetics, supplements and over-the-counter medicines: must carry NAFDAC registration where required, be within date and be correctly labelled
- Alcohol: only by licensed Sellers, never to buyers under 18
- Electronics with lithium batteries: must meet courier rules for battery shipping
- Professional services that need a licence (for example medical, legal or financial services): the licence must be provided during verification

We may remove listings, withhold payouts, suspend accounts and report matters to the authorities for breaches of this policy. If you see a prohibited listing, please report it through the [Contact page](/contact).
