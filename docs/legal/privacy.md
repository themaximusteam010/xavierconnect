**Effective date:** 26 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data XavierConnect collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights under the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023** (NDPA) and related regulations.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

**Xavier Enterprise**, a Nigerian business, operates XavierConnect and is the **data controller** for the personal data described here.

- Data protection contact: **privacy@xavierconnect.com**
- Data protection lead: our Data Protection Officer, reachable at the address above

When a Seller receives your name, address and phone number to fulfil your order, the Seller also becomes responsible for using that data lawfully, and only to fulfil and support your order.

## 2. What we collect

| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Name, email, phone, password (stored only as a secure hash), Google account identifier if you sign in with Google | You, Google |
| Orders and bookings | Items, prices, delivery addresses, delivery instructions, booking times, order history, returns and disputes | You, Sellers |
| Payments | Payment reference, amount and status as returned by the payment provider. **We never receive or store card numbers, CVVs or PINs.** | Payment provider |
| Delivery | Tracking numbers, delivery events, proof of delivery (name of the recipient), customs information for international parcels | Sellers, couriers, freight partners |
| Messages | Messages and attachments between buyers and Sellers, and the results of automated safety checks | You |
| Sellers only | Business details, identity and business verification documents, payout bank or beneficiary details (stored encrypted, or held by the payment provider with only the last 4 digits kept by us), tax information | Sellers |
| Technical | IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, security logs, cookies (see the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies)) | Your device |
| Support | What you tell us in tickets, contact forms and emails | You |

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. We do not collect sensitive personal data (such as health, religion or biometrics) unless a Seller's verification documents happen to contain it.

## 3. Why we use it and our lawful basis

| Purpose | Lawful basis (NDPA s.25) |
|---|---|
| Create and run your account; process orders, payments, deliveries, returns, refunds and payouts | Performance of a contract |
| Verify Sellers, prevent fraud, money laundering, scams and off-platform payment requests, and keep the platform secure | Legal obligation and legitimate interests |
| Scan messages and attachments automatically for contact details, payment requests, scams and abusive content before delivery | Legitimate interests (protecting users from fraud) and contract |
| Keep financial and tax records | Legal obligation |
| Customer support and dispute resolution | Contract and legitimate interests |
| Service emails (order updates, security alerts) | Contract |
| Marketing emails | Consent — you can withdraw it at any time |
| Improve the platform using aggregated statistics | Legitimate interests |

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have weighed them against your rights, and you can object (see section 8).

## 4. Automated checks

Messages and attachments are checked automatically before they are delivered. Messages that contain phone numbers, emails, bank details, links or requests to pay outside the platform may be blocked or held for review. Repeated confirmed violations can lead to a temporary messaging restriction. A person on our trust and safety team reviews flagged content, and you can ask for a human review of any restriction by contacting support. Staff can read only flagged messages or conversations that a participant has reported. There is no general access to private conversations.

## 5. Who we share data with

We share personal data only as needed for the purposes above:

- **Sellers**: your name, delivery address, phone number and order details, to fulfil your order.
- **Payment providers** (for example Paystack): to process payments, refunds and Seller payouts.
- **Couriers, delivery aggregators and freight partners** (for example Shipbubble, GIG Logistics, DHL, FedEx, and our China–Nigeria freight partners, when used): names, addresses, phone numbers, parcel contents and customs information needed to deliver your order.
- **Service providers** who host and operate the platform for us under contract, including cloud hosting and database providers (Vercel, Supabase) and our email provider. They may use the data only on our instructions.
- **Google**, if you choose to sign in with Google.
- **Authorities and others**, when the law requires it, to respond to valid legal requests, to prevent fraud or harm, or to enforce our terms.
- **A buyer of our business**, if all or part of it is sold or reorganised. Your data would remain protected under this policy.

**We do not sell your personal data.**

## 6. International transfers

Our hosting and database providers store data in data centres in the **United Kingdom (London)**. Some service providers may process data in other countries. When personal data leaves Nigeria, we rely on the safeguards in Part VIII of the NDPA, such as contractual clauses with our providers and the protections available in the destination country. Contact us for more information about these safeguards.

## 7. How long we keep data

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| Account data | While your account is open, then up to 2 years after closure (for disputes and fraud prevention), unless a longer period applies below |
| Orders, payments, payouts and ledger records | 6 years after the transaction, for tax and accounting obligations |
| Seller verification documents | While the Seller account is open, plus 6 years |
| Blocked message text and flagged attachments | Deleted after 90 days, unless needed for an open investigation or legal claim |
| Other messages | While the conversation's accounts are active, plus 2 years |
| Security logs | Up to 12 months |

When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

## 8. Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to:

- **access** your personal data and receive information about how it is processed;
- **correct** inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **erase** data we no longer have a lawful reason to keep;
- **restrict** processing in certain circumstances;
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
- **data portability**, meaning a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
- **not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing** that significantly affect you, and to ask for human review;
- **complain** to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission** (ndpc.gov.ng).

To use these rights, email **privacy@xavierconnect.com** from the email address on your account. We may need to verify your identity. We will respond within **30 days**. Some data must be kept even after an erasure request, for example financial records we are legally required to keep, or data needed for an open dispute.

## 9. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), encrypt bank and payout details and provider credentials at rest, hash passwords, require two-step verification for staff, restrict staff access by role, and keep audit logs of sensitive actions. No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, and you where required, as the NDPA requires.

## 10. Your responsibilities

Keep your password secure. Do not share other people's personal data in listings, reviews or messages. Sellers must use buyers' data only to fulfil and support their orders, and must not use it for marketing or any other purpose.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. We will notify you of significant changes by email or on the site.
