description: The rules for behaviour, reviews and messaging on XavierConnect.

**Effective date:** [TO COMPLETE]

## 1. Keep transactions on XavierConnect

Buyer protection works only for payments made through XavierConnect. You must not:

- share or ask for phone numbers, emails, social media handles, bank details or payment links in order to deal outside the platform;
- ask anyone to pay by bank transfer, cash or another app instead of checkout.

Anyone who asks you to pay outside XavierConnect may be trying to scam you. Please report them.

## 2. How messaging protection works

- Messages and attachments are checked automatically **before they are delivered**. Messages that contain contact or payment details, suspicious links or abusive content may be blocked or held for review, and the recipient will not see them.
- Images and files are checked for hidden text and QR codes containing contact details.
- Each confirmed violation is recorded. After repeated violations (currently 3 within 30 days), messaging is restricted temporarily (currently 24 hours). Further violations can lead to an account review, suspension or closure.
- Staff can read only flagged messages or conversations that a participant has reported. There is no general monitoring of private conversations.
- If you think content was blocked by mistake, contact support to ask for a review.

## 3. Respect other users

Do not harass, threaten, insult, discriminate against or defraud anyone. Do not send spam, unsolicited marketing, or content that is sexual, violent or hateful.

## 4. Honest reviews and listings

Reviews must reflect a real purchase and be honest. Do not offer rewards for reviews, review your own items, or post fake or competitor reviews. Listings must be accurate.

## 5. Platform security

Do not try to access accounts or data that are not yours, test or break our security, scrape or overload the site, or use bots without our written permission. Report security issues to support@xavierconnect.com.

## 6. Enforcement

We may remove content, block messages, restrict features, withhold payouts during an investigation, suspend or close accounts, and report illegal activity to the authorities. Where lawful, we will tell you what action we took and how to ask for it to be reviewed.
